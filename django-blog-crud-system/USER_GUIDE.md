# User Guide

## Getting Started

### Registration
1. Click the **Register** button in the top navigation
2. Fill in the registration form:
   - Choose a unique username
   - Enter a valid email address
   - Create a strong password
   - Confirm your password
3. Click **Create Account**

### Login
1. Click the **Login** button
2. Enter your username and password
3. Use the eye icon to show/hide your password
4. Check "Remember me" for persistent login
5. Click **Login**

## Using the Profile Dropdown

After logging in, you'll see your profile icon in the top-right corner.

### Profile Menu Options
- **Home**: Return to the main blog feed
- **Dashboard**: View your posts and statistics
- **Edit Profile**: Update your profile information
- **Logout**: Sign out of your account

## Creating Posts

### From Dashboard
1. Click on your profile icon
2. Select **Dashboard**
3. Click the **Create New Post** button
4. Fill in the post form:
   - Title (minimum 5 characters)
   - Content (minimum 20 characters)
5. Click **Publish Post**

### Post Title Tips
- Keep titles concise and descriptive
- Maximum 200 characters
- Use engaging language

### Content Guidelines
- Minimum 20 characters required
- Use line breaks for readability
- No HTML tags needed (preserved automatically)

## Managing Your Posts

### Viewing Your Posts
1. Access your **Dashboard** from the profile menu
2. All your posts are displayed as cards
3. Each card shows:
   - Post title
   - Content preview (first 15 words)
   - Creation date
   - Update date (if modified)

### Editing a Post
1. Click on a post from your dashboard or home page
2. Click the **Edit Post** button (only visible for your posts)
3. Modify the title or content
4. Click **Update Post**

### Deleting a Post
1. Open the post detail page
2. Click the **Delete Post** button (only visible for your posts)
3. Confirm the deletion

## Browsing Posts

### Home Page Features
- **Search**: Find posts by title, content, or author
- **Sort**: Order posts by newest, oldest, or alphabetically
- **Pagination**: Navigate through multiple pages of posts

### Reading Posts
1. Click on any post title to read the full content
2. Use the **Back** button in the top-left to return to the home page
3. View author information and timestamps

### Author Pages
1. Click on any author's name
2. View all posts by that author
3. See author statistics

## Navigation Tips

### Back Button
- Always located at the top-left of post pages
- Click to return to the home page
- Has a smooth hover animation

### Breadcrumb Trail
- Shows your current location: Home > Author > Post
- Click any breadcrumb to navigate quickly

## Dashboard Statistics

Your dashboard shows:
- **Total Posts**: All posts you've created
- **Posts This Week**: Posts created in the last 7 days
- **Recent Activity**: Number of recently updated posts

## Tips for Best Experience

1. **Use descriptive titles** to attract readers
2. **Write quality content** with proper formatting
3. **Update posts** when information changes
4. **Check your dashboard** regularly for statistics
5. **Search and explore** other authors' posts

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Ctrl/Cmd + F`: Search on home page
- `Esc`: Close dropdown menus
- `Tab`: Navigate through form fields

## Mobile Usage

The blog system is fully responsive:
- Touch-friendly navigation
- Optimized layouts for small screens
- Swipe gestures supported
- Fast loading on mobile networks
